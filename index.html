the mechanism

Fees in as ZEC, zkSNARKs kept.

Six steps, each one either on chain or written down with a signature next to it. Read them and you know everything the strategy is allowed to do.

01

  1. 01The pair is ZEC

    $ZKSTRAT does not trade against SOL. Its pool is quoted in ZEC, which means the fee it pays is already the asset the reserve wants. There is no swap step to trust, no route to slip and no price to get right.

  2. 02Every trade pays the reserve

    Each buy and each sell pays a creator fee straight into the pool. That fee is the only income this thing has — no presale, no emission, no second revenue line.

  3. 03The fee is claimed, not waited on

    A keeper claims it every few minutes, signing with the coin's own wallet. Every claim is written down with its signature, so the schedule is auditable rather than promised.

  4. 04The ZEC sits in one address

    Claimed ZEC goes nowhere. It stays in the wallet that claimed it, which is why a single Solscan page proves both halves of the loop: the fee arriving and the reserve growing.

  5. 05The ZEC bids blind

    When the pile clears the price of a zkSNARK, it goes to the blind auction and names what it will pay. No bid is public before settlement, and every winner pays the lowest successful bid.

  6. 06Nothing is sold

    Whatever clears is posted here the same day and never leaves. The reserve has no sell rule: ZEC is not taken out, zkSNARKs are not flipped, and the only number that is supposed to move is ZEC per million $ZKSTRAT.
